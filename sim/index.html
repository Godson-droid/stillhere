STILLHERE

A dead-man's-switch vault on Tari Ootle. The chain watches the heartbeat, so no one else has to.
SIMULATION · NO WALLET · NO REAL FUNDS
CURRENT EPOCH 0
On chain, epochs advance on their own (roughly 20 minutes each) and the template reads Consensus::current_epoch(). Here you advance time by hand to walk the vault through its life.

1 · Create the vault

Creation mints exactly one owner badge to the owner. The badge is the only key: whoever holds it can check in, withdraw, change the beneficiary, or close. Minting is locked forever.

2 · Act as

Try it: act as the Stranger and press Check in. The template's access rules refuse, exactly as they do on chain. Then let the deadline pass and release as the Stranger: that one is allowed, and the money still lands with the beneficiary, never the caller.

Guided run

Create, top up, check in, miss the deadline, release. About 15 seconds, narrated in the event log.

This page is a simulation of the StillHere template logic. It runs entirely in your browser: no wallet, no chain, no real funds. Every rule enforced here, including every refusal message, mirrors src/lib.rs of the template, and the same behaviours are proven against the real Ootle engine by the test suite in tests/test.rs (run cargo test). On chain, release deposits the full balance directly into the beneficiary's account component, so the person who triggers it can never divert the funds. Simulation written for the Tari November Build Contest.
